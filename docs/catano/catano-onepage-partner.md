## 2. Platform Strategy
**Every visitor gets a phone-first concierge. Every merchant gets a storefront, an audience, and a checkout.** I've already built the engine that runs my white-label networks. Cataño is the destination we point it at.

- **Website & mobile app** — A no-app-needed web platform: live map, merchant directory, ticketed experiences, the Cataño Passport, and an offers wallet — all on the phone they're already holding.
- **Tourist acquisition & engagement** — Capture them at the moment of intent. A QR at the ferry, at Casa Bacardí, at the cruise pier becomes an instant itinerary — then SMS and push keep working them with offers while they're still on the island.
- **Revenue model** — Three stacked meters: recurring merchant memberships, per-transaction ticket commissions, and sponsored placement fed by national ad demand from my exchange — the stream nobody else can bring.
- **Integrating promos, events & attractions** — One feed. A merchant's freebie, a museum's timed ticket, a boxing match, a Bacardí tour — all bookable in the same wallet.

## 3. Merchant Recruitment Program
**We don't sell software to merchants. We bring them tourists with money already in motion.** The pitch is simple: you only show up to people who are already here and already spending.

- **Target categories** — Restaurants and bars, rum / coffee / artisan retail, tours and water excursions, attractions and museums, transport, lodging — starting with the cluster within walking distance of the ferry and Casa Bacardí.
- **Onboarding process** — A 10-minute signup: claim your listing, add photos, a menu, and an offer, go live the same day. White-glove for the first cohort, then self-serve.
- **Membership pricing & levels** — Tiered like my existing ZIP model — Free → Featured → Sponsored — so every merchant can start for nothing and upgrade the moment they see foot traffic.
- **Value proposition** — "Be the freebie they redeem, the table they book, the tour they take." We convert passing cruise traffic into paying customers they'd never have reached.

## 4. Revenue Streams & Pricing
**Two engines: experiences and exposure.** One scales with visitors, the other scales with merchants. We own both meters and tune each independently.

- **Ticketed vs. advertising** — Ticketed = museum admissions, attractions, tours, events; commission per transaction. Advertising = memberships, featured listings, sponsored promos; merchants pay for visibility whether or not a ticket sells.
- **Merchant memberships** — Tiered monthly, mirroring my proven subscription ladder, scaled to a local SMB: free / Featured / Sponsored. (Exact prices tuned with you before launch.)
- **Featured listings** — Flat monthly add-on for top-of-category placement and map-pin priority — the difference between being found and being first.
- **Sponsored promotions** — Pay-to-boost a specific offer or event, sold by campaign or by impressions through the exchange — including outside ad dollars routed onto local inventory.
- **Ticket sales & commissions** — ~10–20% per ticket. We handle checkout, QR redemption, and payout — attractions get a dashboard instead of a spreadsheet.

## 5. Merchant Marketing Services
**Turn every merchant into a media company they don't have to staff.** I already run the ad-exchange and content pipeline; merchants just plug in. The menu:

- **Featured thumbnails & listings** — Premium imagery, top placement, category dominance.
- **Promotional offers & freebies** — "Free shot of rum, free coffee, 2-for-1" redeemable in the Passport wallet — the hook that pulls cruise traffic off the pier.
- **Video tours** — Short vertical walkthroughs we produce and distribute across the network and social.
- **Social media promotion** — Cross-posted to the destination's channels with merchant tagging.
- **Influencer marketing** — Activations with Ivan and a local creator roster, driving audiences into the Passport.
- **Event promotion** — Push a merchant's event into the live feed and out as a notification to everyone on-island.
- **Loyalty & rewards** — The Passport doubles as a loyalty wallet — stamps, repeat-visit rewards, win-back.

## 6. Tourism Experience Development
**The Cataño Passport turns a 90-minute Bacardí stop into a half-day in Cataño.** Collect stamps, unlock rewards, spend more, stay longer. It's the spine tying merchants, attractions, and tickets together.

- **Cataño Passport & roadmap** — A digital passport: check in at stops, redeem offers, earn rewards. The product that gives a visitor a reason to keep moving through town.
- **Walking tours & self-guided experiences** — Mapped routes (rum, food, waterfront, art) with turn-by-turn directions and offers along the way. Zero staff to run.
- **Influencer partnerships, incl. Ivan** — Ivan as the face of the launch — a content series, "Ivan's Cataño," driving his audience into the Passport on day one.
- **Cruise-passenger excursions** — Pre-bookable mini-excursions sized to a cruise window (3–4 hrs): ferry over, Casa Bacardí, lunch with offers, back to the ship with money spent in Cataño.

## 7. Strategic Partnerships
**Anchor on the names that already pull people to Cataño — then be the layer that monetizes the foot traffic they create.** We capture and extend demand; we don't manufacture it.

- **Bacardí** — Casa Bacardí is the magnet. Every tour ticket hands off to a Passport loaded with offers for the rest of the day — we extend the visit beyond the distillery and make Bacardí the front door to the town.
- **Cruise lines & shore-excursion operators** — Get our half-day excursions onto shore-ex manifests; partner on the ferry and transport legs so the trip is seamless gangway to gangway.
- **Local attractions, museums & cultural orgs** — Onboard as ticketed inventory. They get a real booking and redemption system they don't have today.

## 8. Government & Economic Incentives
**Build this inside Puerto Rico's incentive structure from day one.** The tax code and the municipality should be co-investors, not bystanders.

- **Export Services tax incentives** — Structure the platform entity under Act 60. Services sold off-island — national ad demand and SaaS — qualify for the reduced rate. Locked in at formation.
- **Municipal support** — Position the platform as economic development for Cataño: more foot traffic, merchant revenue, jobs. The ask in return — co-marketing, pier and ferry signage rights, event support.
- **July meeting (City Planner + leadership)** — Walk in with this deck, a live demo on a phone, and the first merchant cohort already signed. Goal: a memo of support plus signage and permitting cooperation.

## 9. Destination Development
**Give cruise passengers a reason to choose Cataño over staying in Old San Juan.** Culture, a fight night, a festival, a reason to come back — the more reasons we manufacture, the longer they stay and the more they spend.

- **Culture, entertainment & sporting events** — Curate a recurring calendar — live music, food nights, cultural showcases — all bookable in-app.
- **Boxing events & spectator attractions** — Puerto Rico is a boxing nation. Recurring fight nights become a marquee draw — ticketed, sponsored, promoted across the network.
- **Additional experiences & attractions** — Waterfront, art, rum and coffee trails — scored on draw versus cost; build the winners.
- **Reasons to stay & spend** — The whole game: convert a Bacardí day-trip into a Cataño day. Passport + offers + excursions = longer dwell, more dollars left on the island.
