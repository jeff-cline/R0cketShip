# The Cataño Project — Operating Node Proposal

*Puerto Rico Masterminds · An operating division of R0cketShip Holdings*
*Live deck: `puertoricomasterminds.com/Catano` (password: `JeffCline`)*

> **The thesis.** The Cataño Project is a revenue-generating operating node of R0cketShip Holdings, run locally as Puerto Rico Masterminds. It turns cruise traffic into a managed destination economy — standing on a holding company's master tech stack, capital, demand-generation engine, and shared services rather than building from zero.

---

## 1. Project Structure & Relationship
**The Cataño Project launches as a revenue-generating operating node of R0cketShip Holdings.** Not a startup from zero — a new division standing on a holding company's tech stack, capital, demand-generation engine, and shared services, operated locally as Puerto Rico Masterminds.

- **Proposed business relationship** — An operating division under R0cketShip Holdings, run on the ground as Puerto Rico Masterminds. Holdings contributes the licensed tech stack, demand-gen engine, and growth capital; the local partner contributes market access, merchant relationships, and municipal standing — a joint venture under a licensing agreement.
- **Roles, responsibilities & ownership** — Shared-services companies — PR.TECH (platform), PR.NET (ad network & connectivity), Roatan Sales Team (demand gen), PR.FUND (capital) — deliver infrastructure under license. The local entity owns ground operations, merchant onboarding, government relations, and events. Terms set in the LOI.
- **Timeline & target launch** — Sign the LOI in June → stand up the Cataño node on the master stack and onboard the first cohort by the July municipal meeting → public launch timed to cruise season.
- **LOI & formal contract** — A one-page LOI this month (scope, JV split, Cataño / PR exclusivity), followed by a full operating and licensing agreement consistent with the Holdings structure.

## 2. Platform Strategy
**The Cataño node runs on R0cketShip's master tech stack — proven infrastructure, not a custom build.** PR.TECH's one-for-many SaaS platform already powers every division; Cataño licenses and themes it for a destination economy — live on day one, zero infrastructure risk.

- **Website & mobile app** — Delivered on PR.TECH's one-for-many platform: phone-first web app — live map, merchant directory, ticketed experiences, the Cataño Passport, offers wallet.
- **Tourist acquisition & engagement** — Powered by the Roatan Sales Team demand-gen engine and PR.NET's integrated ad network: capture at point of intent (QR at ferry / Bacardí / pier), then retarget by SMS and push at reduced acquisition cost.
- **Revenue model** — Three institutional revenue lines into the division P&L: recurring merchant subscriptions, transactional ticket commissions, advertising / sponsored placement via PR.NET. Surplus recycles through PR.FUND.
- **Integrating promos, events & attractions** — A single, centrally administered feed across the node, all transacting on the shared platform.

## 3. Merchant Recruitment Program
**Merchant acquisition is run as a managed demand-generation program — not ad-hoc sales.** The Roatan Sales Team and PR.BIZ business-development playbook give Cataño a repeatable acquisition engine.

- **Target categories** — F&B, rum / coffee / artisan retail, tours and water excursions, attractions and museums, transport, lodging — sequenced outward from the Casa Bacardí and ferry walking core.
- **Onboarding process** — Standardized onboarding on the shared platform: claim, configure, go live same-day. First cohort white-glove with shared-services support, then self-serve.
- **Membership pricing & levels** — A tiered subscription ladder (Free → Featured → Sponsored) consistent with Holdings' proven SaaS pricing model, localized for PR SMBs.
- **Value proposition** — Access to managed cruise-and-tourist demand a single merchant cannot generate alone — backed by a holding company's marketing infrastructure, not a lone vendor.

## 4. Revenue Streams & Pricing
**Two clean revenue engines — experiences and exposure — consolidated into one division P&L.** Tickets scale with visitors; advertising scales with merchants. Capital and reinvestment managed through PR.FUND.

- **Ticketed vs. advertising** — Ticketed (admissions, attractions, tours, events) = transactional commission. Advertising (memberships, featured listings, sponsored promos) = recurring visibility revenue via PR.NET. Reported as separate lines.
- **Merchant memberships** — Tiered monthly per the Holdings SaaS ladder, localized. (Price points finalized pre-launch.)
- **Featured listings** — Flat monthly placement add-on for top-of-category position and map-pin priority.
- **Sponsored promotions** — By campaign or impressions, fulfilled through PR.NET's integrated ad network — including external ad demand routed onto local Cataño inventory.
- **Ticket sales & commissions** — ~10–20% per transaction; checkout, redemption, and payout on the shared platform; attractions get a reporting dashboard.

## 5. Merchant Marketing Services
**Every merchant gains a holding-company-grade marketing stack, delivered as a service** through the division and PR.NET — media, content, and distribution no independent merchant could afford.

- **Featured thumbnails & listings** — Premium imagery and top placement on the shared platform.
- **Promotional offers & freebies** — Redeemable through the Passport wallet — the conversion hook for cruise traffic.
- **Video tours** — Produced and distributed across PR.NET's network and social.
- **Social media promotion** — Cross-posted across the destination's owned channels with merchant tagging.
- **Influencer marketing** — Activations with Ivan and a local creator roster, coordinated through PR.BIZ.
- **Event promotion** — Injected into the live feed and pushed to visitors on-island.
- **Loyalty & rewards** — Passport-based loyalty: stamps, repeat-visit rewards, win-back.

## 6. Tourism Experience Development
**The Cataño Passport is the division's flagship product — and its core mandate: cruise-boat optimization.** Extend dwell time, capture local spend. The Passport is the system of record for visitor engagement across the node.

- **Cataño Passport & roadmap** — A digital passport: check in, redeem, reward — tying merchants, attractions, and tickets into one managed visitor journey.
- **Walking tours & self-guided experiences** — Mapped, offer-linked routes (rum, food, waterfront, art) on the platform with no incremental staff.
- **Influencer partnerships, incl. Ivan** — Ivan as the face of the launch; an "Ivan's Cataño" content series funneling audience into the Passport.
- **Cruise-passenger excursions** — Pre-bookable 3–4 hr excursions sized to the cruise window — the operational core of the cruise-boat optimization mandate.

## 7. Strategic Partnerships
**Anchor on demand the destination already generates — and institutionalize it at the holding-company level.** Bacardí and the cruise lines create the traffic; the division captures and extends it with Holdings-level credibility.

- **Bacardí** — Casa Bacardí is the anchor. Each tour ticket hands off into the Passport, extending the visit into a managed itinerary and making Bacardí the front door to the town.
- **Cruise lines & shore-ex operators** — Place excursions on shore-ex manifests; partner on ferry and transport — pursued with the standing of a holding company.
- **Local attractions, museums & cultural orgs** — Onboarded as ticketed inventory on the shared platform.

## 8. Government & Economic Incentives
**Structured from inception inside Puerto Rico's incentive framework.** Act 60 export-services treatment, Opportunity Zone qualification, and municipal partnership designed in at formation — consistent with Holdings' M&A criteria (OZ-qualified preferred).

- **Export Services incentives** — Entity structured under Act 60; off-island services (advertising, SaaS) qualify for the reduced rate. Aligned with the Holdings M&A criteria.
- **Municipal support** — Positioned as economic development for Cataño (foot traffic, merchant revenue, jobs) in exchange for co-marketing, pier and ferry signage rights, and event support.
- **July meeting (City Planner + leadership)** — Present this deck, a live platform demo, and a signed first cohort. Objective: a memo of support plus signage and permitting cooperation.

## 9. Destination Development
**The division actively programs Cataño as a managed destination economy — not a single attraction.** Every new reason to visit is evaluated against the Holdings return criteria, then built. Core KPI: dwell time and local spend.

- **Culture, entertainment & sports** — A curated, recurring calendar — live music, food nights, cultural showcases — bookable on the platform.
- **Boxing & spectator attractions** — Recurring fight nights as a marquee, ticketed and sponsored draw promoted across the network.
- **Additional experiences** — Waterfront, art, rum and coffee trails — each scored against the Holdings ROI / M&A criteria; winners built.
- **Reasons to stay & spend** — Convert Bacardí day-trips into Cataño days — longer dwell time, more dollars retained on the island.
