# Wave-1 Ad Creative Briefs — First 4 Niches

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Audience:** Marketer / creative director
**Use:** Hand this to your designer + copywriter Monday morning. Each niche has 9 ready-to-shoot ad concepts (3 hooks × 3 visual treatments), channel allocation, image briefs (for designer or AI generation), and A/B test priorities.

---

## Creative master rules (apply to all 4)

1. **Hook in 3 seconds** — first line of copy must signal niche + outcome ("Solar installers:" / "Commercial roofers:" / "Mortgage brokers:" / "Agencies:")
2. **One promise per ad** — never combine the data offer with the booking offer in the same creative; let the funnel sort
3. **Proof beats prose** — every ad shows either a specific number, a verbatim testimonial, or a screenshot of the platform delivering value
4. **CTA verb is always action-led** — "Claim", "Lock", "Reserve", "Start" — never "Learn more" or "Click here"
5. **Visual hierarchy:** hero image/video → headline → 1 sentence → CTA button. No more than 4 elements on screen.
6. **Brand inheritance:** every ad ends with the r0cketship rocket lockup in the lower-right + niche brand wordmark in the lower-left
7. **Compliance footer (small text):** "DNC-clean. TCPA/GDPR/CCPA compliant. r0cketship platform."

## Reporting cadence

- **Daily during launch week** (Day 1–7): CPL, signup rate, first-purchase rate
- **Weekly thereafter**: full funnel report — ad spend → impressions → clicks → CPL → signups → first-purchase → Tier-2 upgrades
- **Decision gates**: Day 14 (kill if CPL > 3x target), Day 28 (kill/scale per playbook), Day 56 (final kill or commitment to dedicated SDR + 5x ad budget)
