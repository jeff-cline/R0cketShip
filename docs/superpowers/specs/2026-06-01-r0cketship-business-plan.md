## Executive Summary

r0cketship is a vertical-AI sales operating system for the ~1,200 micro-industries that ZoomInfo, Apollo, and Clay overlook. We don't sell raw data — we sell **booked meetings and closed revenue**, branded vertical by vertical, on a shared data backbone funded by a $100k/yr predictive-intent stack.

Parent brand r0cketship.com is already positioned around predictive intent + ZIP exclusivity + door-knock-optimized lead delivery + CRM sync (HubSpot, GoHighLevel) for contractors and service operators, with a $50 free-credit lead magnet and an E-Partnership program. The strategy below extends that proven engine into 12 named SKUs and a long-tail micro-niche universe of ~1,030+ sub-niches.

**Target outcome:** $100M ARR within 3 years, $1B valuation at 8–10x revenue, ~2,400 customers at $42k blended ACV.

## Branding & Website Architecture

**Parent brand:** r0cketship.com — rocket motif (favicon `/rocket.png`), "Your unfair advantage," predictive intent + ZIP exclusivity + done-for-you positioning. Modern dark/tech aesthetic with ⚡ and ◎ symbol vocabulary.

**Sub-brand strategy:** Each SKU gets its own domain (e.g., solarsignal.com, commercialroofing.pro) inheriting:
- Rocket lockup variant + niche-specific color accent
- Same hero pattern: "High-intent [NICHE] customers actively looking — in your exclusive ZIP, delivered daily to your CRM"
- Same lead magnet anchor: "$50 in free leads / first 25 free"
- Same CRM sync language (HubSpot, GHL, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho)
- Same 3-tier pricing ($1,500 / $4,500 / JV)

Reason: niche domains 3–5x conversion lift on paid ads + better SEO + buyer trust ("Built for me, not for everyone").

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Data source dependency (Apollo, others) | Diversify across 5+ sources; build first-party intent capture via lead magnets |
| Compliance (TCPA, GDPR, CCPA, DNC) | DNC-scrub is core IP; legal review per vertical (especially healthcare, financial) |
| AI booking agent quality | Start with human-in-the-loop, ramp automation per niche; track booking-to-show rate |
| Channel concentration (Meta / LinkedIn algorithm shifts) | Diversify channels per SKU; build SEO long-tail asset per niche |
| Cannibalization between SKUs | Disciplined ICP definition per brand; share-of-buyer audit quarterly |
| JV partners default on revenue share | Standardized contracts; advance payment + monthly reconciliation; cap exposure |
