# Wave-1 SDR Call Scripts — First 4 Niches

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Audience:** SDR team / sales manager / call coach
**Use:** Daily working scripts for SDRs dialing inbound + outbound leads. Each script has a master opening, niche-specific qualifiers, anchor pitch on the $4,500 booking tier, 5 niche-specific objection handlers, and the booking ask.

---

## Universal opening (use across all niches)

**3-second hook (earn the right to keep talking):**
> "Hi, this is [SDR NAME] from r0cketship. We delivered 50 free [NICHE] leads to your account this morning — I'm calling to make sure they were the right fit and to see what kind of pipeline you're trying to fill. Got 30 seconds?"

**If they say "yes" / "go ahead"** → proceed to qualifying
**If they say "no" / "busy"** → "Totally fair. When's better — later today or tomorrow morning?" (book a callback in the dialer)
**If they say "what's r0cketship?"** → "It's the predictive lead platform you signed up for — you got 50 free leads to test. I'm just making sure they're the right quality before you decide whether to keep going."

---

## Universal call structure (5–7 minutes target)

1. **Opening hook** (3 seconds)
2. **Permission** (30 seconds — buy time)
3. **Qualifying** (3 questions, ~90 seconds)
4. **Pitch anchored on $4,500** (60 seconds)
5. **Handle objections** (variable)
6. **Booking ask** (30 seconds — every call ends with calendar attempt)

If they're not buyer-fit: book a 15-min meeting anyway with the closer for qualification, OR mark as dead in the dialer with a reason code.

---

## Universal qualifying logic (adapt 3 questions per niche)

These three questions decide tier placement:

- **Q1 — Volume/scale:** Establishes their existing pipeline size and confirms they're a real buyer, not a tire-kicker
- **Q2 — Outcome math:** Calculates value-per-deal so you can show ROI on Tier 2
- **Q3 — Capacity:** Determines if they have closers (Tier 1 ok) or need closers (Tier 2/3)

The script per niche fills in Q1/Q2/Q3 with niche-relevant numbers.

## 1. SolarSignal — Solar/HVAC/Roofing SDR Script

**Buyer:** Owner-operator residential solar/HVAC/roofing companies, $500k–$5M revenue

### Niche-specific qualifying

**Q1 (volume):** "How many installs are you doing per month right now? And what's your funnel — door-to-door, ads, referrals?"

**Q2 (outcome math):** "What's your average installed system price and your commission per close?" *(typical: $25k–$45k system, $2k–$5k installer net)*

**Q3 (capacity):** "Who's doing the dialing today? You, in-house reps, or are you outsourcing?"

### Niche pitch (anchor on Tier 2)

> "Here's how this works for installers like you. The $1,500 plan gives you the data — your ZIP exclusively, 50–200 fresh intent-scored homeowners a month, synced to GoHighLevel or HubSpot, DNC-clean. Most installers start here.
>
> But the customers we work with who 5x their installs are on the $4,500 plan — we actually book the consultations on your calendar. You show up, they're warm, system info already in the notes, and you close. At your $3k avg commission, you need ONE extra install per month to make $4,500 back. Most of our solar customers book 6–12 extra consultations per month at our calendar.
>
> Which sounds closer to where you are — running your own dial team, or wanting someone else to do it?"

### Niche objection handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I already use AngiList / Modernize / Sunrun leads" | "Those leads are sold 5–7 times. Ours are exclusive to your ZIP. Big difference — your competitor literally cannot have them." |
| "I have a guy who door-knocks" | "Perfect. Our leads are ZIP-targeted intent — give your knockers a list of addresses where the homeowner already searched for solar this week. Same crew, way better hit rate." |
| "How do I know the leads are real?" | "Easy. Your $50 signup credit is already loaded — pull 10 right now, dial them while we're on this call. If you don't get a live person in the first 5, we'll comp another 50." |
| "I can't afford $4,500" | "Then start at $1,500 and upgrade when you've closed your first deal from the leads. That's 2 weeks for most of our installers." |
| "Send me info first" | "Of course. I'll send the one-pager right after we book a 15-minute walkthrough with our closer — Tuesday at 2pm or Wednesday at 10am?" |

### Booking ask
> "Let's get 15 minutes on your calendar to walk through your specific ZIP, your current install volume, and which plan makes sense. Tuesday 2pm or Wednesday 10am — which works?"

### Disposition guide
- **booked** — calendar invite sent (target: 10–15% of connected calls)
- **hot_transfer** — they want to talk to closer NOW; transfer to hot_transfer_number
- **callback** — set callback time in dialer
- **left_message** — voicemail dropped, mark for retry tomorrow
- **no_answer** — retry pattern: 4 hours later, then 24 hours, then 48 hours, then dead
- **dead** — explicitly not a fit (wrong niche, too small, hostile)
- **sold** — they bought during the call (rare, but happens — close in dialer w/ sale value)

## Universal closing rules (across all niches)

### Every call ends with one of three states
1. **Booked** (best) — calendar invite in their email before you hang up
2. **Callback set** — specific time, in the dialer, follow-up email auto-sends
3. **Dead** — explicit reason code (wrong niche / hostile / not decision-maker / etc.)

### Never:
- End a call with "I'll send you info" without a booked follow-up
- Promise pricing or features outside the published tiers
- Discount or negotiate — that's the closer's job
- Argue with prospects — book the meeting and let the closer handle it

### Always:
- Use the prospect's name twice (start and during booking ask)
- Confirm the calendar invite landed before hanging up
- Note 1–2 personal/business details in the dialer for the closer
- Mark disposition in the dialer within 60 seconds of hang-up

## SDR daily targets (Week 1–4 per niche)

| Metric | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Dials/day | 50–80 | Twilio click-to-call from agent dashboard |
| Connect rate | 15%+ | Below 10% = data quality issue, escalate |
| Booking rate (from connected calls) | 8–15% | Below 5% = script issue, retrain |
| Show rate (booked → attended) | 50%+ | Below 40% = booking quality, add reminder cadence |
| Daily hot transfers | 1–3 | Direct lift on closer pipeline |

---

## Coaching loop

- **Daily**: SDR reviews disposition tape with manager (5 min/day)
- **Weekly**: Top 3 calls and bottom 3 calls reviewed in team meeting; script tweaks based on common objections
- **Monthly**: Refresh objection handlers based on what's actually killing deals; update qualifying questions based on what predicts closed business

---

## When to escalate to the closer

- Prospect explicitly asks pricing detail beyond what's published
- Prospect mentions a competitor by name and wants direct comparison
- Prospect asks about JV / revenue share — always escalate immediately
- Prospect is C-level at a 50+ person company — hot transfer or executive briefing
- Prospect mentions a specific dollar threshold ("if you can do $1M for me, I'll sign today")
